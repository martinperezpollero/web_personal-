Between 2016 and 2020, Daniel Pena and I put together a play-music duo. Our
work unfolded along two lines. On one hand, arrangements of popular music
and original compositions meant to bring out the power of wind and string
instruments. On the other, the musical experience «entre nosotros» (roughly,
"among ourselves"): a research space we built by touring house livings, to
explore different forms of interaction between the musicians and the
people taking part, between play and music.

We performed, among other venues, at the Festival de Escenas Breves at
INAE-Casa INJU, at Música por la Vida at the Fundación Zelmar Michelini, and
with [*Micropoesías de la vida cotidiana*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1VHNOPFJvEI)
at El Chamuyo. We also took part in the short films
[*Encerrados*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AN7aM5arzxo) (Casa Bertolt
Brecht) and the spot
[*No a la baja*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FTv6-hMNyhs) by Colectivo
Catalejo.
