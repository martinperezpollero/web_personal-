Between 2024 and 2025 we co-designed Kit lúdico Colateral between the
Municipality of Montevideo and the Universidad Católica del Uruguay: a set
of tools for drug-use prevention with adolescents. My role on the team was
game design and project management.

We built it as five box games, with progressively difficult levels, meant
for sustained groups of adolescents aged 12 to 18: school groups or teams
who already know each other and share a common activity.

For the design we reviewed national and international prevention
programmes, looking to strengthen decision-making, resistance to peer
pressure, and reflection on one's own environment. The Municipality of
Montevideo ran a pilot programme over the 2025–2026 summer (December to
March); the launch was covered by
[Canal 10](https://www.canal10.com.uy/colateral-prevencion-uso-sustancias-adolescentes-n998170).
