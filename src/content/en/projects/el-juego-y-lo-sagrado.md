Between 2020 and 2022, José Pablo Velázquez and I put together *El juego y
lo sagrado* (roughly, "Play and the Sacred"): a workshop that set out to
explore the power of play as a political device for transforming reality.

We started from the idea that the economy, the law, politics, and other
forms of communication and bonding are confined to the realm of the sacred
and become untouchable. Profaning those dimensions, from Giorgio Agamben's
perspective, is a way of returning the sacred to the human sphere, and so
unleashing the power of transformation.

We were looking for a space where the act of profaning, through play, would
open up new forms of communication, bonds, and territories.
