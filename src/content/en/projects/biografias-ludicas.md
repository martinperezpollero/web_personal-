I'm working on my PhD dissertation about how playing — and giving up play —
takes part in shaping who we are: a qualitative study, done with young
Uruguayans, that doesn't take for granted that play is innocent, but sees
it as shaped by gender, the market, and the digital, and holds onto that
critical lens throughout.

That research also produces a digital annex: the material that doesn't fit
in a book. Interactive visualizations of participants' play trajectories,
their digitized fanzines, audio excerpts from the interviews, and the
phrase-cards each person wrote about their own play.
